# Story bible template

Write `<game>/docs/story-bible.md` with these headings, in this order. It is the single source of truth: every card is written from it, and every card is checked against it. Keep it in plain prose and tables.

## Brief
One line per interview answer (theme, tone, guests, characters, hosts and pronouns, difficulty, length, language, voice, lights, props, location, party date).

## Open items
Every answer still missing, the default you built with, and who owes it (for example "Party date: not given. Assumed none; print deadline unknown. Ask at hand-over.").

## Theme facts
What the web search confirmed or corrected: dates, technology, laws, money, slang. Note anything you changed to fit.

## Backstory
How the victims, the killers and the place are connected. What happened years ago.

## The two murders
For each: killer, motive, method, weapon, where the weapon ends up (which picture envelope), the moment it happened, what the killer did right after, and what the killer will lie about.

## How the victims speak
Ghosts, or the theme's alternative (story-format.md › Supernatural or not), and how Ghost B rises after 2.3.

## Timeline
Minute by minute from arrival to the second murder: who stood where, who poured or carried what, how many glasses, who left the room. Every card is checked against it.

## Cast
| id | Name | Costume | Pronouns | Role (killer, obvious suspect, witness, herring, comic) | Team | Optional | Secret (2.1) | Goal (2.2) | Public act and quirk |

Every innocent has a real 2.1 secret and a 2.2 goal they can pursue at the table.

## The killers' sign
Phrase, answer, the innocent who says the phrase too, and confirmation that both halves and the instruction to answer are on both 2.2 cards.

## Clue matrix
| Clue | Points to | Stage | Source (card, strip, narration, set piece) | Backup source if that guest is absent | Shared with (the innocent it also fits) | Separated at stage |

## Red herrings
Each herring: what makes them look guilty, and the secret that explains it.

## Torn document
Every strip's number, holder and text. Strip 1 is Ghost A's note; the last is Ghost B's.

## Team sentences and pictures
For each team at 3.1 and 3.2: the words in order with their holders, the picture, what the envelope holds, and each killer's wrong word with the picture it points to. Both decoys.

## Witness cards
Reader, backup ghost, and text, per team.

## Alibis
Every alibi. For each killer: the lie and the two earlier cards that contradict it (rule 3). Confirm no card before 3.3 disproves a killer's sabotage lie (rule 6).

## The box
Hiding place, where the true key sits and in what, the fake keys, the innocents' hints split into what and where, the killers' opposite orders, and the letter.

## Table talk and whispers
Not every line, but the plan: who raises what about whom at each stage, each card's always-printable last line (no subject, or about a killer), and which whispers hint at whose secret.

## Props
Every prop named in the story, who gets it and when. Must match interview question 11.

## Calibration log
Each playtest round: date, playtesters' characters, top-two and confidence by stage, what confused them, which knobs you turned.
